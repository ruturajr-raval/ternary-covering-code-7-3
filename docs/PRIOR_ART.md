# Prior Art

Status date: 2026-09-05

## Current Interval

Gabor Keri's ternary covering-code table records

```text
11 <= K_3(7,3) <= 12.
```

Source:

- https://old.sztaki.hu/~keri/codes/3_tables.pdf

The table attributes the historical upper bound to:

- I. S. Hämäläinen and S. Rankinen, "Upper Bounds for Football Pool
  Problems and Mixed Covering Codes", Journal of Combinatorial Theory,
  Series A 56 (1991), 84-95.
- https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(91)90024-B

A later construction and verification reference is:

- I. S. Hämäläinen, S. Rankinen, and R. Östergård, "New Upper Bounds for
  Binary and Ternary Covering Codes", Designs, Codes and Cryptography 14
  (1998), 87-96.
- https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1008228721072

## Recent Bounds

The 2026 semidefinite-programming computation reports `8.5250` for this cell,
which yields only the integer lower bound 9 and does not improve the table's
lower bound 11.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01932

No primary-source result located through 2026-09-05 settles or improves the
`11..12` interval.
